# Privacy Policy — KaamWala

**Effective date:** [DATE] · **Operator:** [YOUR LEGAL ENTITY NAME], Pune, Maharashtra, India
**Contact:** [YOUR-EMAIL] · [YOUR-PHONE]

KaamWala ("we") connects customers with independent service professionals
(plumbers, electricians, painters, carpenters) in Pune. This policy explains
what personal data we collect and how we use it, in line with India's Digital
Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

## 1. Data we collect

| Data | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number | All users | Login via one-time password (OTP); booking coordination |
| Name, city, profile photo | All users | Profile display; worker verification |
| Booking address + service details | Customers | So workers can perform the booked job |
| Aadhaar scan (KYC) | Workers only | Identity verification before approval (stored in a private storage bucket, never public) |
| Portfolio photos | Workers | Showcasing work to customers |
| Payment records (booking fee ₹20, payouts) | All users | Processed by Razorpay; we store only order/payment references, never card or UPI credentials |
| Device push-notification token | All users | Delivering booking/job notifications |
| Crash logs & usage analytics (Firebase) | All users | Fixing crashes, improving the app |

## 2. What we do NOT do
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not read your SMS, contacts, or files beyond what you explicitly upload.
- Card/UPI details are entered in Razorpay's secure checkout — they never reach our servers.

## 3. Sharing
Data is shared only with: (a) your counterparty in a booking (name/photo so you
know who is coming); (b) Razorpay (payments); (c) Google Firebase
(notifications/analytics); (d) Supabase (infrastructure/hosting); (e) law
enforcement where legally required.

## 4. Retention
Booking and payout records are retained as required by Indian tax/accounting
rules. Worker KYC documents are retained while the worker account is active.
You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at
[YOUR-EMAIL]; statutory retention requirements apply.

## 5. Your rights (DPDP Act)
Access, correction, and erasure requests: email [YOUR-EMAIL]. You may withdraw
consent for notifications via device settings; withdrawal of essential-data
consent ends your ability to use the service.

## 6. Security
All traffic uses TLS. KYC scans live in a private, access-controlled bucket;
database access is role-restricted with row-level security. Payments are
handled by a PCI-DSS compliant gateway (Razorpay).
