# Refund & Cancellation Policy — KaamWala

**Effective date:** [DATE]

## Customer booking fee (₹20)
| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| You cancel while booking is **pending** (no worker accepted yet) | **Automatic full ₹20 refund** — initiated instantly, credited back to your original payment source typically within 5–7 working days (bank/UPI dependent) |
| Worker declines or does not accept | Fee is refunded automatically |
| Worker accepts, then you cancel | Contact support: [YOUR-EMAIL] — reviewed case-by-case; if the worker hasn't started travel the fee is normally refunded |
| Job completed | No refund of booking fee |
| Payment succeeded but app showed failure | Auto-detected via payment webhook and refunded/linked automatically |

## Worker payouts
Payouts (90% share) are released to the worker's registered bank/UPI account
after customer confirmation. Failed transfers are retried; unresolved after 3
attempts are escalated to support.

## How refunds are processed
Refunds go back through Razorpay to the **original payment instrument only**.
We never refund to a different UPI ID/account.
